# TASK: meca_verify
# version: 1

For each numbered claim below, decide one thing only: **is the claim supported
by the quoted evidence?**

You are not judging whether the claim is true in the world, interesting, or
well written. You are judging whether someone reading only the quoted evidence
would agree the claim follows from it.

CLAIMS:
{claims}

Answer `false` when:
  the evidence does not mention the subject the claim names;
  the claim states a time the evidence does not support;
  the claim asserts as fact something the evidence only proposes, wishes for or
    asks about;
  the claim adds a detail -- a place, a person, a quantity -- absent from the
    evidence;
  the evidence quotes text that does not appear to be a real excerpt.

Answer `true` when the evidence plainly supports the claim, including when it
supports it with ordinary reading rather than word-for-word repetition.

Return JSON: {{"verdicts": [{{"id": 1, "supported": true}}, ...]}}
One entry per claim, in the same order. No explanation.
