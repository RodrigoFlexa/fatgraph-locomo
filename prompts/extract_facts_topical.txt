# TASK: extract_facts
# version: 2-topical

You are extracting atomic memories from one session of a long-running
conversation between {speaker_a} and {speaker_b}.

DATE OF THIS SESSION: {session_date}

TRANSCRIPT (each line is prefixed with its turn id):
{transcript}

Extract every atomic fact that is worth remembering long-term. Rules:

1. One fact = one relation between exactly TWO entities.
2. Facts must be SELF-CONTAINED: resolve every pronoun and every ellipsis using
   the session context. A reader who sees only `fact_text` must understand it.
3. `fact_text` is ONE sentence naming the people involved by their proper names
   -- never "I", "you", "he", "she", "they". If {speaker_a} or {speaker_b} is
   part of the fact, name them in this sentence.

4. CHOOSING THE TWO ENTITIES -- read this carefully, it is what differs from a
   naive extraction:

   `entity_1` and `entity_2` are the two things the fact CONNECTS. When the
   sentence involves a speaker AND two other things, connect THOSE TWO, and
   leave the speaker to `speaker` and to `fact_text`.

   Example. From "I found the transgender stories at the support group really
   inspiring", said by Caroline:

     WRONG   entity_1="Caroline"           entity_2="transgender stories"
     RIGHT   entity_1="transgender stories" entity_2="LGBTQ support group"
             speaker="Caroline"
             fact_text="Caroline found the transgender stories at the LGBTQ
                        support group inspiring."

   The wrong version loses the only link that lets a later question travel from
   the support group to what was said there. The speaker is recorded either way.

   Attach a fact to a speaker ONLY when the fact is genuinely about that person
   and no second topic exists: "Caroline is transgender", "Jon lives in Seattle".
   Those are correct as `entity_1="Caroline"`.

5. Prefer entities that RECUR. If the session already gave you "LGBTQ support
   group", reuse that exact string rather than "the group" or "a support group
   downtown". Two memories about the same thing must name it identically, or
   they will not connect.

6. `entity_1` and `entity_2` are short canonical noun phrases (a person, place,
   organisation, event, object, activity, preference). Never a whole sentence,
   and never a possessive wrapper around a speaker when the thing itself will
   do: prefer "camping trip" over "Melanie's camping trip".

7. `relation` is a short lowercase verb phrase: "took place at", "led to",
   "is part of", "reminded of", "happened before".

8. `speaker` is whoever said it: exactly "{speaker_a}" or "{speaker_b}".

9. `turn_ids` lists every turn id that supports the fact (usually one or two).

10. Do NOT invent anything. Do NOT extract greetings, small talk or questions
    that carry no durable information. Include dates and quantities that appear.

11. If a turn only shares an image, use its caption as the content.

Return STRICT JSON, no prose, in this shape:

{{"facts": [
  {{"entity_1": "...", "relation": "...", "entity_2": "...",
    "speaker": "...", "fact_text": "...", "turn_ids": ["D1:3"]}}
]}}
