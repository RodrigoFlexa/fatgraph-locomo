# TASK: meca_infer
# version: 1

You are given a passage and the claims already extracted from it. Write down
what a careful reader would additionally KNOW after reading it -- the things
that follow from what was said, that were not said outright.

PASSAGE DATE: {date}
PASSAGE:
{passage}

ALREADY EXTRACTED:
{known}

Good inferences, with the reasoning made explicit:
  "I'm moving to Lisbon in April" (said in March)
      -> subject lives in Lisbon, valid_from 04 of that year, modality asserted
  "I finally finished the thesis"
      -> the subject was writing a thesis before that date
  "we're back from Kyoto"
      -> the subject was in Kyoto, valid_to the passage date

Bad inferences, which you must not make:
  guessing a cause that was not stated
  guessing how someone felt
  turning a plan into an accomplished fact when the passage gives no evidence
    that it happened
  restating a claim that is already in ALREADY EXTRACTED

Use the same fields as the extraction step. Every inferred claim must still
carry `evidence`: the exact substring of the passage it follows from, copied
character for character.

Add one more field:
  because      one short sentence saying why this follows.

Rules:
1. Infer only what the passage forces. If a reasonable person could read it and
   not conclude your claim, do not write it.
2. Return an empty list when nothing follows. That is the common case and it is
   a correct answer.
3. Never contradict a claim in ALREADY EXTRACTED.

Return JSON: {{"propositions": [ ... ]}}
