# TASK: extract_facts
# version: 1

You are extracting atomic memories from one session of a long-running
conversation between {speaker_a} and {speaker_b}.

DATE OF THIS SESSION: {session_date}

TRANSCRIPT (each line is prefixed with its turn id):
{transcript}

Extract every atomic fact that is worth remembering long-term. Rules:

1. One fact = one relation between exactly TWO entities.
2. Facts must be SELF-CONTAINED: resolve every pronoun and every ellipsis using
   the session context. A reader who sees only `fact_text` must understand it.
3. Use the speakers' proper names, never "I", "you", "he", "she", "they".
4. `entity_1` and `entity_2` must be short canonical noun phrases (a person, a
   place, an organisation, an event, an object, an activity, a preference).
   Never a whole sentence.
5. `relation` is a short lowercase verb phrase, e.g. "works at", "adopted",
   "feels about", "visited", "plans to".
6. `fact_text` is ONE sentence, past or present tense, stating the fact with
   both entities named. Include dates or quantities that appear in the turn.
7. `turn_ids` lists every turn id that supports the fact (usually one or two).
8. Do NOT invent anything. Do NOT extract small talk, greetings or questions
   that carry no durable information.
9. If a turn only shares an image, use its caption as the content.

Return STRICT JSON, no prose, in this shape:

{{"facts": [
  {{"entity_1": "...", "relation": "...", "entity_2": "...",
    "fact_text": "...", "turn_ids": ["D1:3"]}}
]}}
