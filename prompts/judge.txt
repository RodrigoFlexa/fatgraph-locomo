QUESTION: {question}

Decide whether the CANDIDATE conveys the same information as the REFERENCE.

Count it CORRECT when:
- it states the same fact in different words ("counseling" vs "a counselor");
- it is more specific than the reference but consistent with it;
- it gives the same date, quantity or name in another format
  ("8 May 2023" vs "May 8th, 2023", "two" vs "2");
- it adds a brief correct justification the reference omits.

Count it INCORRECT when:
- it states a different fact, name, date or quantity;
- it says the information is absent while the reference gives an answer;
- it answers a different question than the one asked;
- it is so vague that it does not commit to the reference's claim;
- it lists several alternatives without choosing, and the reference chooses;
- it contradicts the reference in any part, even if another part matches.

Judge ONLY whether the candidate matches the reference. You do not have the
conversation, and you must not reason about whether the reference is itself
right. Wording, length, style and punctuation are irrelevant; only the
information counts. When genuinely undecidable, answer false.
